seep Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

숮맣혓 ("we") operates seep, a wishlist app (the "app"). This policy explains what personal information we process and how we protect it, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

At a glance

  • If you don't sign in, your wishes stay on your device and are never sent to a server.
  • Signing in is optional. If you sign in, your wishes are stored on a server (Google Firebase) so you can see them on your other devices.
  • Deleting your account immediately deletes your data and photos on the server.
  • We don't collect advertising identifiers, and we never sell your information or use it for ads.

"Letters to future me" stay hidden in the app until the day you sealed them for, but they are stored on the server without end-to-end encryption. Security rules on the server let only your own account read and write your data.

2. Retention and deletion

  • Data you store on the server by signing in is kept until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your data and photos on the server are deleted without delay.
  • When you delete a wish, only a deletion marker is kept so your other devices know it was deleted, and it is fully removed from the server after 90 days. Photos of deleted wishes are deleted right away.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are deleted automatically after Google's retention period (up to 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics).
  • Data on your device is deleted when you delete the app, or when you choose "Delete from this device" when signing out.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered. We don't keep personal information on paper.

4. Your rights

  • You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information at any time.
  • In the app: edit or delete wishes, or go to My → Account → Sign out (with the option to delete data on this device) or Delete account.
  • For anything else, email us at the address below and we'll handle it without delay. A legal representative or someone you authorize can also make a request.
  • Children under 14 should not use the sign-in feature, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

5. Security

  • All communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Security rules let only you, when signed in, access your server data. Administrative access is limited to those who need it.
  • App Check blocks requests that don't come from the genuine app.
  • If you turn on App Lock (Face ID or passcode), the app verifies it's you when it opens.

6. Automatic collection

The app doesn't use web cookies. The Firebase SDK stores an app installation ID on your device for usage statistics; it is removed when you delete the app.
